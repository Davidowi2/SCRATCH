- KERNEL: S-006
- BATCH: B-004 (FINAL YouTube-backlog batch, Owner Ruling 5)

- INSTRUMENT/TF: EURUSD H1, Dukascopy pipeline, Gate 1 validated dataset
- SESSION FILTER: none (all hours)
- MECHANISM: trade pullback re-entries in the direction of a confirmed
  EMA50/EMA200 regime. Hypothesis: trend-following with distance-filtered
  regime beats a no-regime baseline net of friction.

## MATH (LOCKED)

1. **EMAs**: recursive EMA, period 50 and 200, SEEDED with the SMA of the
   first N bars of the evaluated window (window = phase-filtered bars; the
   runner passes only IS or OOS bars). Declared: seeding is per-phase,
   deterministic, and no bar outside the window influences the EMAs.
2. **Regime at bar i**:
   - UPTREND: EMA50[i] > EMA200[i] AND (EMA50[i] - EMA200[i]) > 0.2 * ATR14[i]
   - DOWNTREND: mirrored (EMA200 > EMA50 AND distance > 0.2*ATR14)
   - SIDEWAYS (distance <= 0.2*ATR14): NO trades in either direction.
3. **Entry LONG at signal bar i**: regime UPTREND AND close[i-1] < EMA50[i-1]
   AND close[i] > EMA50[i] (pullback re-cross of the fast EMA).
   SHORT mirrored: DOWNTREND AND close[i-1] > EMA50[i-1] AND close[i] < EMA50[i].
4. **Entry price**: open of bar i+1. One position at a time; one trade per
   signal event (no re-trigger on the same cross).
5. **Stop**: distance 1.5 * ATR14[i] measured at the signal bar, applied from
   entry (long: stop = entry - 1.5*ATR14[i]; short: mirrored).
6. **Target**: 2.0R (R = the stop distance).
7. **ATR14**: bars up to and including i — S001/S010 lineage convention
   (mean of true ranges over i-13..i). Declared because the repo holds two
   ATR conventions (S012 uses preceding-only); this kernel uses the EURUSD
   lineage's inclusive form.

## FILL CONVENTIONS (FACTORY-WIDE)

- **R1**: stop evaluated before target every bar; one bar touching both =
  LOSS; an open gapping through the stop fills at the open; max-hold checked
  after stop/target, at the close.
- **R3**: entry bar open gapping through the planned stop (risk <= 0)
  discards the signal entirely. Flat-bar ATR=0 cases (1,272 OHLC-flat bars
  exist in the 2020 smoke slice) hit risk<=0 and discard — by design.
- B3 lineage: entry bar IS evaluated (i += 1 after trade creation).
- B4 lineage: gross metrics from pre-friction outcomes.
