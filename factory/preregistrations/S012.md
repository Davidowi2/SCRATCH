## MECHANISM

Bank-level momentum breakout, crypto adaptation (S-010 lineage, Owner Ruling 4).
The first 25 minutes of each UTC day form a "bank range"; the following hour is
where the day's first institutional push is expected. Hypothesis: a
volatility-expansion close beyond the opening bank range inside the first hour
continues into the remaining minutes before the 01:30 hard flat.

## MATH (LOCKED — CARD VERBATIM)

1. **Bank range** = high/low of 5m bars stamped 00:00–00:25 UTC inclusive
   (bars :00, :05, :10, :15, :20, :25).
2. **Trigger window** = bars stamped 00:30–01:25 UTC inclusive.
   (Note: bank ends :25, window begins :30 — every bar is accounted for.
   There is NO unclaimed gap bar, therefore NO M4 pre-window violation rule
   is needed; the first window bar's prior close is the 00:25 close, which is
   inside the range by construction.)
3. **Signal**: FIRST bar j in window with close[j] beyond the bank range AND
   close[j-1] not beyond (cross-event rule: first crossing bar only)
   AND (high[j] − low[j]) ≥ 1.0 × ATR14(5m), ATR computed over the 14 bars
   PRECEDING j (indices j−14..j−1; simple mean of true ranges — same
   convention as S-008/S-010 lineage).
4. **Entry**: open of bar j+1. LONG on upside break, SHORT on downside break
   (first break direction of the day wins). ONE trade per UTC day max.
5. **Stop**: opposite extreme of the trigger bar j (long → low[j];
   short → high[j]).
6. **Target**: 1.5R.
7. **Hard flat**: close of the bar stamped 01:30 UTC. Whichever comes first
   among stop / target / hard-flat (R1 ordering).
8. **Friction** = 2 × 0.0005 × entry_price, computed PER TRADE from that
   trade's entry price, in points (taker fee 5 bps each leg). Binance futures
   klines are last-trade prices; the fee term is the modeled cost floor —
   documented, not hidden.
9. **24/7 rule**: any day missing at least one bar in the span
   00:00..01:30 (the mechanism's required clock) is SKIPPED entirely.
   No partial-day ranges, no substitutions.

## FILL CONVENTIONS (FACTORY-WIDE)

  LOSS; open gapping through stop fills at the open; hard-flat checked after
  stop/target, at that bar's close.

  DISCARDS the signal entirely. No trade, no deferral.
- B3 lineage: the entry bar IS evaluated (i += 1 after trade creation).
