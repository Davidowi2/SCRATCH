## MATH (LOCKED)

1. **Regression zone at bar i**: 50-bar linear regression of CLOSE over
   window i-49..i (least squares, x = bar index). Fitted value at x=i is the
   midline; bands = midline +/- 2.0 * std-dev of the RESIDUALS over the same
   50-bar window. (std = population std sqrt(mean(res^2)) — declared.)
2. **Stochastic (14,3,3) standard**:
   - rawK[i] = 100*(close[i]-min(low[i-13..i]))/(max(high[i-13..i])-min(low[i-13..i])),
     0 if denominator 0 (flat-window guard).
   - K = SMA3 of rawK (simple, over i-2..i); D = SMA3 of K (over i-2..i).
   - INTERPRETATION NOTE: the source video's "custom settings" were never
     stated. Standard (14,3,3) is preregistered as the declared
     interpretation; deviation recorded per Owner Ruling 5.
3. **Entry LONG at bar i**: close[i] <= lower band AND K[i] < 20 AND
   K crosses above D: K[i-1] <= D[i-1] AND K[i] > D[i].
   ("Cross back inside" operationalized as the K/D cross while K is in the
   oversold zone — declared interpretation, preregistered.)
   SHORT mirrored: close[i] >= upper band AND K[i] > 80 AND
   K[i-1] >= D[i-1] AND K[i] < D[i].
4. **Entry price**: open of bar i+1. One trade per signal event; one position.
5. **Stop LONG**: min(low[i-9..i]) - 0.5*ATR14[i] (swing-low anchor).
   **Stop SHORT**: max(high[i-9..i]) + 0.5*ATR14[i].
6. **Target**: 2.0R measured from entry with R = |entry - stop|.
   R3: if entry open leaves risk <= 0 (gapped through the anchor stop), the
   signal is discarded entirely.
7. **ATR14**: S001/S010 EURUSD lineage convention (inclusive at signal bar).
